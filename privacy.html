Roots of War Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.rootsofwar.game (Raíces de Guerra in Spanish)

Roots of War is a free, ad-supported, single-player tower defense game. This policy describes exactly what data is collected and who receives it. It was written after reviewing the code that ships, not before: if something is not listed here, the app does not do it.

The short version. The game asks for no account, no email, no sign-up. Your progress is stored only on your phone and is never uploaded to any server of ours — we do not run servers. The only data that leaves your device is collected by three Google services: ads, usage analytics, and crash reporting.

1. Data the app stores on your device

Stored locally, in the phone's own storage, and never transmitted:

  • Campaign progress: which maps you finished and with how many stars.
  • Permanent progress: coins, gems, unlocked plant species, chosen branches and the levels of your permanent upgrades.
  • The state of the run in progress, so you can pick it up again.
  • Your endless-mode record.
  • Your daily missions and the date of the last one, so they are not handed out twice.
  • Your preferences: language, music, sound effects and game speed.

None of this identifies a person and none of it leaves the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Data collected by third-party services

The app integrates three Google services. Google collects that data directly and it is governed by their own policies, linked below.

Google AdMob — advertising

We show two kinds of ad: rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit, and ads between runs. To serve and measure them, AdMob may collect:

DataPurpose
Device advertising identifierChoosing which ad to show, capping repeats, measuring results
Device information (model, operating system, language)Delivering a compatible ad
IP address, from which an approximate location is derivedRegion-appropriate ads and per-country legal compliance
Ad interactionsMeasurement and fraud prevention

You can reset or delete the advertising identifier at any time from Settings → Google → Ads on your phone, and turn off personalization in the same place.

Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics

We collect aggregated, nameless statistics to know whether the game works: how many people open it, which map they stop playing on, which country and which phone model. The service generates a random installation identifier — not your name, not your email, not your Google account — and records automatic events such as first open, session start and engagement time.

It exists so we know what to fix. If one map makes half the players quit, this is the only place that shows it.

Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports

When the app fails, an automatic report is sent so it can be fixed. The report includes the error type, the device model, the operating system version and a random identifier generated by Crashlytics for that installation.

We also attach game context to reproduce the problem: which map you were on, which wave, how many enemies were on screen and the game version. Those are numbers from the run. No personal data, contacts, location or device content is attached.

3. What the app does NOT do

  • It does not ask for or store your name, email, phone number or any contact detail.
  • It uses no accounts, no sign-in and no social networks.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • It does not record what you type or tap outside the game itself.
  • We do not sell data to anyone. We have no data to sell.

4. Children

Roots of War is directed at people 13 and older and is not designed for children under that age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

5. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in one of those regions, before the first ad you will see a consent message managed by Google's User Messaging Platform. There you choose whether you accept personalized ads. The choice is saved and can be changed again from that same message.

6. In-app purchases

This version has no purchases. Everything unlocked in the game is paid for with currency earned by playing. No payments are processed, no payment method is requested and no billing data is involved.

If purchases are added in the future, Google Play will process them: we would never receive or store your payment details, only the confirmation that a purchase completed. This page would be updated before that happens.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data from Android settings. The game itself also includes a Delete progress button. Data collected by AdMob, Analytics and Crashlytics is retained according to the Google policies linked below; you can exercise your rights over it directly with Google.

8. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page is updated and the date in the header changes. Important changes are also announced inside the game.
